Privacy policy

Suryanamaskar uses your phone's camera only to detect your body position while you exercise, so that it can count your suryanamaskars.

  • The camera picture is analysed on your phone, frame by frame, and then discarded.
  • No photo or video is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
  • The app has no account, no advertising, and no analytics.
  • Your daily counts and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app does not collect or share any personal data.

Questions: open an issue on the app's GitHub page.
